## How we choose our sources

We rely in our articles on official parties, real estate development companies' websites, banks, and published contracts and documents, and when we mention a number or an official procedure, we try to put its source at the end of the article, so the reader can refer to it and verify it himself.

## When we update articles

Prices, laws, and procedures change, so we review articles from time to time, and write the date of the last update at the beginning of the article.

## Correcting errors

If you notice an error in any article, send it to us from the "Contact us" page with the article link and the intended information. We review it, and if the error is confirmed, we correct it and update the article date.

## Limits of content

What we publish is general information for awareness, and is not legal, financial, or engineering advice, and before any purchase or signing decision, consult the official party or a specialist.
