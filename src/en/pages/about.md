«Your Property from Afar» is an Arabic website that explains to Egyptians abroad and inside Egypt the steps of buying a property, following it up, and managing it, in a simple language and without complication.

## What we offer

We write practical articles about the Egyptian real estate market: how to choose the area, what documents are required, and what to pay attention to before paying or signing.

## How we work

We choose our topics from the questions posed by property owners and buyers, and we write in a direct style that suits those who do not know the market well.

## Warning

The information on the website is for general awareness, and is not a legal or financial consultation. Verify the numbers and procedures from the official party before making any decision.

For any comment or correction, contact us from the «Contact us» page.
