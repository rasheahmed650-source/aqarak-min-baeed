## What we collect

The site does not ask you to create an account, the "Contact Us" form does not send your data to the site; it prepares a WhatsApp message that you send from your application.

## Hosting

The site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages, and Cloudflare may retain technical data about the visit, such as the IP address, for security and operation purposes. For details: [Cloudflare Privacy Policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).

## WhatsApp and contact

When you press the WhatsApp or contact button, you are transferred to an external application, and your message or call is subject to the policy of that application.

## Contacting us about privacy

For any question or request related to your privacy, contact us from the "Contact Us" page.
