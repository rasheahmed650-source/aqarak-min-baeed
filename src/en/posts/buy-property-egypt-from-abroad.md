Buying a property in Egypt no longer necessarily requires traveling to complete every step of the purchase process, as Egyptians living abroad can now search for residential units, compare projects, review some documents, and communicate with sellers and real estate development companies without being physically present.

However, the ease of communication does not mean that the buying process is devoid of challenges, as choosing a suitable apartment through photos and electronic presentations differs from verifying its actual condition, and signing a sales contract does not necessarily mean that the property transfer procedures are complete.

This guide covers the steps of buying a property in Egypt from abroad, starting from choosing the unit and examining its documents, through power of attorney, contracting, and transferring funds, to registration and handover, while clarifying the most important legal requirements, costs, and risks that need to be taken into consideration.
## Can a property be bought in Egypt without traveling?
Yes, many stages of buying properties in Egypt can be completed while living abroad, but the possibility of completing the process entirely without personal attendance depends on the type of property, the selling party, the method of signing, and the registration requirements.

When buying a unit from a real estate development company, the buyer may be able to view the plans and prices, choose the unit, obtain a copy of the contract, and pay the agreed-upon amounts remotely, whereas when buying an apartment from an individual owner, there may be a need for additional procedures to verify the ownership document and the seller's capacity and sign the necessary documents.

And it is necessary to distinguish between three stages: booking the unit, concluding the sales contract, and transferring ownership or registering the property right.
For booking and contracting can be done remotely, while registration procedures require additional documents or signatures, so it is necessary to ensure the possibility of completing all procedures before paying large amounts, and not just relying on the possibility of electronic booking.
## What are the steps to buy a property in Egypt from abroad?
The buying process starts with determining the purpose of owning the property, then choosing the suitable unit, examining the documents, negotiating the contract terms, until payment, registration, and handover, and it is preferable to follow a clear order so that the buyer does not bear financial obligations before verifying the basic aspects of the deal.
1###. Determining the purpose of purchase and budget
The choice of property varies according to its purpose, as the goal may be to secure housing upon return to Egypt, or owning a unit for vacationing, or investing through rental or resale in the future, and this goal affects the choice of area, type of unit, its area, and finishing level.

As well as defining a budget that includes the property price and expenses associated with the purchase, such as lawyer fees, registration fees, maintenance, bank transfers, and finishing when needed.
2###. Searching for suitable properties
After defining the budget, a list of properties that meet the required needs can be prepared, and it is preferable to compare several units in terms of location, price, area, services, finishing condition, and handover date, with a request for recent photos and clear diagrams.
One should not rely solely on advertising photos, especially when buying a unit that is still under construction.
3###. Reviewing documents before financial commitment
Before signing a binding contract or paying large amounts, it is necessary to use an independent lawyer to examine the basic documents, and the examination includes verifying the seller's identity and legal status, ownership documents, relevant licenses, and the possibility of transferring the contracted right or registering it.
If the reservation requires paying an amount before completing the examination, it is important to review the conditions for refunding it and the obligations that arise from it.
4###. Agreeing on the price and payment terms
A written offer should be obtained that clarifies the total price, down payment, installment schedule, maintenance expenses, handover date, and any additional costs.
It is also necessary to determine what happens if the buyer is late in paying or the seller is late in fulfilling their obligations.
5###. Preparing the power of attorney or signing method
If personal attendance is not possible, it is possible to research issuing a special power of attorney for a person inside Egypt or using another signing method accepted by the concerned parties.
The required authorities should be reviewed before issuing the power of attorney so that they are not wider than necessary or insufficient to complete the procedures.
6###. Paying and documenting amounts
It is preferable to use payment methods that provide clear evidence of the transaction, while ensuring the beneficiary's data and matching it with official documents.
Receipts for payment and transfer notifications should be kept, and the paid amounts should be reviewed with the schedule of obligations stated in the contract.
7###. Following up on registration and handover
After contracting, it is necessary to follow up on the procedures for transferring ownership or registering property rights according to the unit's legal situation.
Upon handover, a technical inspection should be conducted and the actual condition compared with the agreed-upon specifications, and any defects or incomplete work should be documented.
## How do you choose the right property while residing abroad?
Choosing a property from abroad requires researching several aspects, because the photos and offers published on the internet do not always reveal the actual condition of the unit or the nature of the surrounding area.
Therefore, the property should be evaluated according to specific criteria that suit the purpose of the purchase and the available budget.
### Choosing the area according to the purpose of the purchase
If the goal is to live, it is important to study the level of services, proximity to schools and hospitals, means of transportation, and the nature of the area in terms of quietness and population density.

However, if the goal is investment, it is necessary to study the demand for rental, price levels, supply volume, and the possibility of resale in the future.
The rise in property prices in a particular area cannot be considered sufficient evidence of the quality of the investment; the purchase cost may be high compared to the expected rental return.
### Comparing Price to Actual Cost
When comparing properties, one should look at the total amount required and not just the announced price, as two units with the same area may differ in finishing costs, maintenance expenses, handover dates, and payment system.

It is also useful to request a written price quote that includes the area, finishing condition, final price, installments, and additional expenses.
### Conducting an Independent Inspection of the Property
Video calls and recent photos can be beneficial in getting to know the unit, but they do not replace a technical inspection, and it is preferable to hire an independent engineer to inspect the finished property, ensure the integrity of the finishes and facilities, and verify that the area and specifications match.

As for units under construction, it is necessary to follow up on the implementation stages and verify the documents, licenses, and obligations related to handover.
## How to Verify the Property and Seller Before Purchase?
Checking the legal status of the property is a basic step, as the existence of the property in reality does not prove by itself that the person offering it for sale has the right to dispose of it.

Some problems may be related to the lack of clarity in the ownership sequence, the existence of registered restrictions, differences in unit data, or the seller's lack of necessary legal capacity.
### Verifying the Seller's Identity and Capacity
When buying from an individual owner, the buyer's identity data must match the documents on which he relies to prove his ownership.
If the sale is made through an agent, the power of attorney must be reviewed, and its validity, powers, and scope verified to ensure it covers the required transaction.
When buying from a real estate development company, the company's data, the legal representative's capacity, and the documents related to its right to develop the project and sell its units must be reviewed.
### Checking Ownership and Licenses
The legal examination may include reviewing the title deed, previous transaction sequence, registered restrictions, building licenses, and relevant regulatory documents.

It is also necessary to ensure that there are no financial obligations or rights of others that may affect the deal, and a certificate of real estate transactions helps to identify some transactions and registered restrictions, but it is not enough on its own to prove that the property is free from all disputes or violations.
### Signs that warrant caution before contracting
Indicators that deserve attention include the refusal to provide documents, or the existence of a difference between the seller's data and the contract, or the demand for payment of a large amount before legal review.
Also, it is necessary to carefully verify if a request is made to transfer funds to an account whose owner's connection to the seller or the contracting party is not clear.
In these cases, it is preferable to postpone payment and signing until the information is clarified and verified independently.

### Documents for new projects
When buying a unit from a real estate developer, it may be necessary to review the reservation contract, the sale contract, the installment schedule, the land allocation documents, licenses, and delivery and maintenance terms.

In cases of resale within projects, there may be a need for documents of waiver or developer's approval, according to the contract terms and the nature of the unit.
### Organizing documents from abroad
It is preferable to keep clear electronic copies of contracts, receipts, and correspondence, while keeping the originals in a secure manner.
Also, dividing documents into files related to ownership, contracting, payment, and registration helps to facilitate following up on procedures with the lawyer or the seller.
## How are the contracting procedures for the property done remotely?
After choosing the unit and verifying its documents, the stage of reviewing the sale contract and determining the signing method begins, and some real estate development companies may allow sending contracts and completing part of the procedures electronically, but it is necessary to ensure that the signing method is accepted by the party in front of which the contract will be used.
### Reviewing the terms of the sale contract
It is necessary to obtain a complete copy of the contract and its attachments before signing, with a review of the terms related to the parties' data, property description, price, payment schedule, and delivery date.

As well as paying attention to the terms of cancellation, the penalties resulting from delay, the responsibility of the parties for fees and taxes, and the procedures for transferring ownership, it is better to clarify any ambiguous clause in writing before signing, and not to rely on verbal promises.
## Can the contract be signed from abroad?
It may be possible to sign from abroad using a legal means accepted by the competent authority, or through an agent who has the appropriate powers.
But sending a signed contract via email does not automatically mean that all the requirements of contracting or registration have been met, and therefore, it is necessary to agree in advance on the method of signing and adopting documents, especially if they are to be submitted to the Real Estate Registry.
## The difference between a sales contract and property registration
A sales contract determines the contractual obligations and rights between the parties, while the material property rights that require registration are subject to independent registration procedures, and Law No. 114 of 1946 regulates the procedures of the Real Estate Registry, and some of its provisions have been amended by Law No. 9 of 2022.

Simply signing a preliminary contract or obtaining a judgment on the validity of the signature is not a substitute for the registration required to transfer registered property.

The documents and registration procedures differ according to the state of the property and the legal basis of the request, so it is necessary to verify the requirements of the competent department before contracting.
## Do you need to appoint a power of attorney for someone inside Egypt to complete the purchase?
Issuing a power of attorney is not necessary in all cases of buying properties from abroad, but it may be a suitable means when the procedures require the presence of a person inside Egypt.

The power of attorney grants another person the authority to perform specific actions on behalf of the buyer, according to what is stated in its text, and these actions may include signing contracts, submitting documents, following up on registration, or receiving the unit, according to the specified powers and the requirements of the competent authority.
### What type of power of attorney is suitable?
When buying a specific property, it is usually preferable to use a special power of attorney that clarifies the required powers rather than granting the agent general and broad powers.
It is important that the power of attorney includes the data of the parties, a description of the property when available, the permitted procedures, the limits of disposition, and the extent of the agent's right to delegate to others, and it is necessary to avoid adding phrases that allow contracting with oneself or restricting the cancellation of the agency without understanding its legal implications.
### How to issue a power of attorney from abroad
The Egyptian Ministry of Foreign Affairs explains that issuing a consular power of attorney for Egyptians requires an original proof of Egyptian identity that is valid, whether an Egyptian passport or a national number card, with two copies of the identity document and completion of the power of attorney form.
The signature is made before the competent official, and it is not permissible to erase, amend, or add to the power of attorney after it has been issued and received.

The consulate may require additional documents depending on the type of power of attorney and its purpose, and therefore it is necessary to review the instructions of the concerned mission before attending.
As for the powers of attorney issued in front of foreign parties, they may require certifications and certified translations according to the country and the party in front of which they will be used inside Egypt.
### Can the power of attorney be cancelled after it is issued?
The possibility of cancelling the power of attorney depends on its nature, its texts, and the extent of its connection to a legal interest for the agent or others, and the instructions of some Egyptian missions indicate the necessity of submitting a copy of the power of attorney to consider the possibility of cancelling it according to its nature.

For this reason, it is necessary to review the formula of the power of attorney legally before it is issued, especially when there are phrases related to non-cancellation or contracting with oneself.
## How do you pay the price of the property and transfer money from abroad?
Paying the price of the property from abroad requires ensuring the means of payment and the beneficiary's data, in addition to keeping the documents that prove the transfer of the amounts.
The importance of these procedures increases when buying in installments or when there are special requirements to prove the source of the money and the method of transferring it.
### Choosing a documented payment method
Bank transfers help provide a clear record of financial transactions, but it is necessary to verify the beneficiary's name, account number, and the required currency before making the transfer.

When buying from a real estate development company, it is preferable to obtain payment data from the company's official channels, and not to rely on instructions sent from unauthenticated accounts.

It is also necessary to keep the transfer notice and the receipt issued by the beneficiary party, and to match the amount with the obligations specified in the contract.
### Requirements for transferring the price when non-Egyptians own
There are special requirements for some ownership transactions of non-Egyptians for properties in Egypt, as the Information and Decision Support Center of the Council of Ministers clarified that the Real Estate Registry procedures related to selling properties to foreigners residing abroad included the requirement to submit proof of transferring the value of the unit from abroad in foreign currency through the Egyptian banking system, as of March 26, 2024.

This condition is related to non-Egyptian ownership transactions that are subject to the instructions, and it should not be generalized to all Egyptians residing abroad.

Therefore, it is necessary to ensure the bank's and the registration authority's requirements before transferring the price, especially if the buyer does not hold Egyptian nationality.
### Calculating exchange rate differences and transfer fees
When the buyer's income is in a foreign currency while the property price is paid in EGP, the actual cost of the installments may change due to the change in the exchange rate, and bank transfer fees and currency conversion fees may be added to the total cost.

For this, a financial plan should be prepared that takes into account the change in exchange rates, especially when there are installments that extend over several years, and a bank transfer alone is not evidence of the transfer of property ownership; it proves a financial transaction, while proving property rights requires the appropriate documents and legal procedures.
## How do you follow up on the purchase and handover procedures while you are outside Egypt?
The process of buying a property does not end with just signing the contract and paying the down payment, especially if the unit is under construction or the handover date is postponed, and regular follow-up helps to ensure the implementation of contractual obligations and detect any delay or difference in specifications before handover.
### Following up on legal and financial procedures
It is preferable to agree with the lawyer or the person responsible for following up on the deal to provide periodic reports that include the procedures completed, the documents received, and the remaining steps.

It is also necessary to keep a clear schedule of installments, review the payment receipts issued by the developer or seller, and ensure that there are no unjustified amounts.
### Following up on the implementation stages of the property
For units under construction, periodic reports and recent photos can be requested from the project site, and compared with the agreed-upon timeline, however, marketing photos alone are not enough to judge the level of implementation or the quality of work.

It may be appropriate to use an independent engineer to conduct a technical inspection before handover, especially when there are finishing or installation works that need to be inspected.
### Reviewing the unit before handover
When the handover date approaches, the actual condition of the property should be compared with the specifications stated in the contract and its attachments.
The inspection usually includes the area and internal division, the condition of finishes, doors and windows, electricity and plumbing, and the extent of readiness of facilities.

In case of defects or incomplete work, it is preferable to document them in the inspection or handover minutes, with a determination of how to address them, before signing a final receipt without reservations.

### Avoiding Conflict of Interest
Relying on one person for choosing a property, negotiating, checking documents, and following up on payment may lead to a conflict of interest.
Therefore, it is preferable to separate roles whenever possible, so that the broker presents options, an independent lawyer conducts the legal review, and an engineer inspects the technical condition.

### Not Rushing Due to Limited Offers
Some real estate offers may be associated with a short time period or a limited number of units, but this should not push the buyer to bypass basic inspection procedures.

Getting a suitable price does not compensate for the risks resulting from buying a property without verifying its documents or contract terms.

### Considering Rules for Non-Egyptian Ownership
If the buyer is not an Egyptian national, the ownership conditions must be verified before choosing a property or signing the contract.

Law No. 230 of 1996 regulates non-Egyptian ownership of built properties and vacant land, and the general rule in Article 2 includes the possibility of owning two properties at most for the buyer's private residence and their family, provided that the area of each property does not exceed 4,000 square meters, with exceptions and special cases.

Article 5 also includes a restriction on disposing of ownership before five years have passed, with the possibility of exceptions according to the law.

There are special rules for some areas, so it is necessary to review the ownership conditions associated with the property's location and nature, and not assume that all units are subject to the same procedures.

### Real Estate Transaction Tax in Egypt
The real estate transaction tax is applied at a rate of 2.5% of the transaction value in cases subject to it, taking into account exceptions and relevant legal provisions.

The Egyptian Tax Authority announced, as part of the 2026 tax facilitations, the unification of the 2.5% rate for real estate transactions for non-merchants, regardless of the number of transactions, extended the payment deadline to 60 days, and exempted sales between first-degree relatives according to the announced rules.

In addition to the known expenses, it is preferable to allocate a reserve amount to face exchange rate differences, or to extract additional documents, or to handle defects that appear upon handover, and this amount is determined according to the value of the property, its condition, and the obligations associated with it, without assuming a fixed percentage that suits all deals.
