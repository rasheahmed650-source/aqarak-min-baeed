Buying a property in Egypt while residing abroad is a decision that requires a study that goes beyond comparing prices and areas; the buyer faces challenges related to knowing the suitable areas, verifying the integrity of documents, and evaluating the property without direct inspection.

Therefore, knowing how to choose the right property in Egypt while you are abroad starts with understanding the real estate market and determining the purpose of the purchase, whether for housing, rental, or investment, with knowledge of the legal procedures and potential risks before making a decision.

## **Basic Criteria for Choosing the Right Property in Egypt**

Choosing a property depends on a set of criteria that help you determine the unit or land that is most compatible with your needs and budget, and it is not limited to choosing a famous area or a new project, but rather requires studying the purpose of the property, the nature of the location, the total cost, and the extent to which the property is suitable for future use.

### **1. Determining the Purpose of Buying or Renting the Property**

Before starting the search, determine the primary purpose of owning or renting the property; because the needs of the buyer differ from those of the renter and the investor, if you are looking for a property for housing, focus on the area of the unit, the number of rooms, the level of finishing, and its proximity to schools, hospitals, markets, and transportation.

If you want to rent an apartment during your visits to Egypt, focus on the location, amenities, rental period, and contract terms, and how close the unit is to the places you need to visit.

In the case of real estate investment, it is necessary to study the demand for rental, resale opportunities, and expected returns, taking into account maintenance, management costs, and market-related risks.

### **2. Getting to Know the Real Estate Areas in Egypt**

The location is one of the most important factors affecting the value of the property and its suitability for housing or investment, and real estate areas in Egypt vary between existing cities, new urban communities, and coastal areas, each with different characteristics, and among the most prominent areas that can be studied are:

- **New Cairo and Fifth Settlement:** They are characterized by a variety of residential units, commercial and administrative projects, in addition to the presence of axes, roads, educational and medical services.
- **The New Administrative Capital:** It includes various residential, commercial, and administrative areas, and the level of readiness of services and construction varies from one area to another.
- **6th of October City and Sheikh Zayed:** They provide multiple options for apartments, villas, and residential projects, with a variety of educational, commercial, and medical services.
- **The North Coast and Ain Sokhna:** Real estate opportunities are related to seasonal use and accommodation during holidays, and the nature of investment in them differs from permanent residential areas.

The area should not be chosen based on its fame only, but it is necessary to study the level of occupancy, available services, surrounding roads, and actual demand for properties.

### **3. Determining the Budget and Comparing Property Prices**

Among the basic steps when thinking about how to choose the right property in Egypt is determining the available budget, considering that the cost of the property is not limited to the announced purchase price.

When buying an apartment or villa, there may be additional costs such as maintenance fees, finishing, documentation, and registration, in addition to installments and payments due according to the contract system.

As for buying land, it is necessary to study the price per meter, amenities, building requirements, and costs associated with its preparation or development.

It is also important to consider the currency in which you will pay the value of the property, exchange rates, and bank fees, especially if you receive your income in a foreign currency. To make a correct comparison between properties, make sure to do the following:

- Compare units that are close in location, area, and specifications.
- Calculate the total price of the unit, not just the down payment value.
- Review the installment schedule and their due dates.
- Identify maintenance and additional service fees.
- Calculate the costs of finishing and preparation when needed.
- Estimate the expenses of holding and managing the property.

This comparison helps you know the actual value of the property and avoid choosing a unit that seems low in price at first but requires large additional expenses.

### **4. Evaluating the type of property and its suitability for your needs**

The criteria for choosing residential apartments differ from those of villas, lands, and commercial and administrative units. Therefore, it is necessary to understand the nature of each type before making a decision.

- **Residential apartments:** may be suitable for family residence or rental.
- **Villas:** provide larger spaces and higher privacy, but may require additional operating and maintenance expenses.
- **Lands:** it is necessary to study their location, area, permitted use, amenities, and building requirements.
- **Commercial and administrative units:** it is important to evaluate the commercial activity movement, demand for spaces, customer nature, and competition in the area.

The choice of property type should be compatible with the purpose of ownership, not just personal preferences or the project's marketing appearance.

## **How to verify the property before making a purchase decision**

After determining the goal, area, and budget, comes the stage of verifying the property itself. This stage gains special importance when choosing the right property in Egypt while residing abroad, as the buyer cannot follow up on all details himself and needs reliable information and independent inspection before financial commitment. Among the most important aspects are:

### **1. Examining ownership and legal documents**

Verifying the legal status of the property is one of the most important steps that should not be overlooked. Advertisements and pictures are not enough to prove the integrity of ownership or the legality of the building. Among the most important points that need to be reviewed are:

- Property ownership document or land deed.
- Sequence of ownership transfer in case of purchasing from a previous owner.
- Building permits and required approvals.
- The unit's status regarding registration and the possibility of transferring ownership.
- Existence of mortgages, reservations, disputes, or rights of others.
- Financial obligations associated with the property.
- Terms of the sales contract, handover, and payment.

It is recommended to hire an independent lawyer specializing in Egyptian real estate transactions to review documents, contracts, and verify the possibility of completing ownership transfer and registration procedures according to the case.

### **2. Verifying the Real Estate Developer or Seller**

If you are buying a unit within a new project, do not rely solely on advertising campaigns or the company's reputation, but rather search for a record of previous projects and the extent of commitment to handover deadlines and execution quality.

It is also important to identify the company's legal name, its data, and the project's status regarding necessary permits and approvals, in addition to verifying the legal status of the land on which the project is built.

When buying from a direct owner, it is necessary to ensure their identity, legal capacity, and right to dispose of the property.

### **3. Remote Inspection and Evaluation of the Property's Condition**

Your absence from Egypt does not mean waiving the inspection, but rather requires relying on means of verification that help you form a realistic image of the property. You can request a live tour via video call that includes the unit from the inside and outside, with photography of the street, building, facilities, and surrounding services.

The inspection should include the unit's area, room distribution, finishing condition, plumbing quality, electricity, lighting, ventilation, and the condition of shared parts.

In the case of ready or used units, it is possible to hire an independent engineer to examine the technical condition and identify potential defects. For units under construction, it is necessary to follow up on the implementation stages and compare them with the agreed-upon specifications.

### **4. Reviewing the Sales Contract and Terms**

Before signing the property purchase contract, it is necessary to ensure that all data and obligations are clear, and not to be satisfied with only verbal agreements. Among the most important items to be reviewed are:

- Property description, location, and area.
- Total sale price and payment method.
- Handover date and agreed-upon specifications.
- Delay and cancellation conditions and refund of amounts.
- Maintenance fees and financial obligations.
- Buyer's rights and seller's obligations.
- Property ownership transfer and registration procedures.

All important agreements must be in writing, and legal review should be subject to an independent specialist before signing the contract or transferring funds.

## **Procedures for Owning Properties in Egypt for Expats and Foreigners**

The procedures for buying properties in Egypt while abroad vary according to the buyer's nationality, the nature of the property, the selling party, and the method of contracting. Therefore, it is essential to learn about the appropriate procedures for each case before starting to implement the transaction.

### **1. Procedures for Buying a Property from Abroad**

An expatriate can follow up on many stages of research and purchase using digital communication means, such as obtaining property details, comparing prices, conducting photographed inspections, and reviewing documents electronically.

However, it is necessary to ensure the original documents and procedures that require personal attendance or official authorization, according to the nature of the transaction and the competent authority.

It is also advisable to keep organized copies of contracts, correspondence, payment receipts, and all documents related to the purchase process.

### **2. Official Power of Attorney and its Role in Completing Transactions**

For Egyptians living abroad, it is possible to inquire about the possibility of issuing an official power of attorney through the Egyptian embassy or consulate, to authorize a person inside Egypt to execute specific tasks.

These powers may include following up on some procedures, submitting and receiving documents, or signing certain documents, according to what is permitted by the power of attorney and the nature of the transaction.

It is best to define the powers accurately and not issue a general and broad power of attorney without necessity, with a review of the legal wording before its adoption.

### **3. Controls for Foreign Ownership of Properties in Egypt**

If you are a non-Egyptian foreigner and wish to own a property inside Egypt, it is essential to learn about the legal rules governing non-Egyptian ownership.

The conditions may vary according to nationality, type of property, location, and purpose of ownership, and some cases may be subject to restrictions, approvals, and special procedures.

Therefore, it is necessary to refer to the official competent authorities and seek the assistance of a lawyer with experience in foreign ownership transactions, and not rely on general information or the experiences of other people as rules that apply to all cases.

## **How to Evaluate Real Estate Investment Opportunities in Egypt?**

If your goal is to invest in real estate in Egypt, do not make the expectation of rising prices the only criterion for judging the quality of the opportunity, but rather evaluate the property in terms of demand, costs, expected return, and the ease of disposal in the future.

### **1. Study of Rental Demand**

Verify the existence of categories that need housing or use properties in the area, such as employees, students, families, and business owners.

It is also necessary to compare the number of units offered for rent with the actual demand level, as an increase in supply may affect the ease of renting and the rental value.

### **2. Calculation of Expected Investment Return**

The initial annual rental return can be estimated by dividing the total expected annual rent by the total cost of purchasing the property, and then multiplying the result by 100.

However, it is necessary to distinguish between the total return and the net return, the latter taking into account expenses, periods of non-rental, maintenance, management, and other financial obligations.

### **3. Evaluation of Resale Opportunities and Value Increase**

Study the demand level for similar properties in the area, the availability of services and facilities, and the ease of access to the location.

Do not assume that all new areas or modern projects achieve guaranteed price increases, as future value is affected by multiple factors, including supply and demand, quality of implementation, level of services, and economic conditions.

### **4. Identifying Real Estate Investment Risks**

It is essential to study the risks associated with delayed handover, weak demand, high operating costs, or legal problems with ownership.

You should also distinguish between investing in residential apartments, land, and commercial and administrative units; because each type has a different nature in terms of demand, costs, returns, and risks.

## **Here are Practical Tips for Choosing the Right Property in Egypt from Abroad**

It is crucial when choosing the right property in Egypt while you are living abroad to organize the search process and minimize the chances of falling into avoidable mistakes. Therefore, follow these tips:

1. Determine whether your goal is residence, rental, or investment.
2. Choose areas that suit your needs and budget.
3. Gather information about prices, spaces, and types of available properties.
4. Compare between three or more options before making a decision.
5. Verify the real estate developer or seller and their previous record.
6. Request recent photos and videos and a direct inspection.
7. Consult an independent lawyer to review documents and contracts.
8. Understand the legal rules for ownership if you are a foreigner.
9. Calculate all costs and fees before financial commitment.
10. Do not transfer money before ensuring the beneficiary's identity and the agreement's integrity.
11. Keep electronic copies of contracts, documents, and payment receipts.
12. Develop a clear plan to follow up on the property's maintenance and management after purchase.

These steps do not guarantee that the investment is risk-free, but they help you make a more informed decision based on clear information and independent review.

Knowing how to choose the right property in Egypt while living abroad starts with determining your goal, understanding the nature of real estate areas, studying prices, verifying legal documents, and evaluating the property and investment opportunity before making a decision.

Do not let geographical distance be a reason to rely on incomplete information or unconsidered offers.
