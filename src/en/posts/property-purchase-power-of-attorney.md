The Egyptian expatriate needs to know the procedures and legal documents before buying a property in Egypt, especially if they wish to complete some steps without returning to the country. Completing the transaction remotely may require preparing personal documents, examining the property and seller's papers, and issuing a power of attorney to a trusted person who will carry out specific procedures on their behalf.

The importance of knowing the powers of attorney and the required documents for buying a property in Egypt from abroad lies in the fact that any deficiency in the documents or error in drafting the power of attorney may lead to disrupting the procedures or granting the agent powers that the buyer does not need.

In this guide, we review the most important required documents, types of real estate powers of attorney, and the steps to issue and document them from abroad, in addition to how to verify the validity of the power of attorney and property ownership before completing the contract.

## **Powers of Attorney and Documents Required to Buy a Property in Egypt from Abroad**

The required documents vary depending on the nature of the property, the method of purchase, and the party that handles the contract procedures. There are documents that pertain to the buyer themselves, and others related to the property and the seller. Additional documents may be required if the purchase is to be made through an agent within Egypt.

### **Personal Documents Required from the Egyptian Buyer Living Abroad**

It is advisable to prepare the basic personal documents before starting the procedures, so that the data used in the contracts and powers of attorney is consistent. These usually include:

- A clear copy of the valid Egyptian passport.
- A copy of the national ID card, if available and valid.
- Residence data in the country where the buyer lives.
- Address of residence and means of communication.
- Data of the agent to whom the power of attorney will be issued, such as the full name, national ID number, and address.
- Documents proving the capacity if the buyer is acting as a representative of a company or agent of another person.

The Egyptian embassy or consulate may require the original personal identification document and copies of it when drafting the power of attorney. They may also request additional documents according to the nature of the transaction.

### **Property and Seller Documents Before Completing the Purchase**

The property documents are no less important than the buyer's papers, as they help examine the legal status of the unit before paying money or signing the contract. Among the documents that should be requested and reviewed, according to the property's condition, are:

- The seller's title deed or the document that explains the basis of their ownership.
- The previous sales contract or series of contracts, if the ownership has been transferred from more than one person.
- Property data, its location, area, boundaries, and unit or plot number.
- Documents for allocating the land or unit if the property is affiliated with a specific authority or project.
- What indicates the financial obligations and maintenance dues, if any.
- Licenses and documents related to the building, according to the nature of the property and its condition.
- Seller's identification documents and what proves their capacity in the sale.

This list is guiding and not a unified list for all properties; the required documents vary between a registered property, a unit in a project affiliated with a company, and a property that is still being dealt with through consecutive contracts.

### **Additional Documents Required When Buying Through a Power of Attorney**

If the buyer decides to use a person inside Egypt, they will need to prepare additional documents related to the power of attorney, including:

- The original power of attorney or the official certified copy as required by the relevant authority.
- A copy of the national ID card of the attorney.
- The property data subject to the transaction if the power of attorney is specific to a particular property.
- Any additional documents required by the documentation authority, the seller, or the authority with jurisdiction over the property.

It is important to ensure that the wording of the power of attorney allows the attorney to perform the required procedure specifically, so it is not enough to have a general power of attorney if the relevant authority requires a special authority.

## **How to Issue a Real Estate Power of Attorney from Abroad to Complete a Purchase in Egypt?**

The power of attorney is one of the legal means that allows the buyer to delegate another person to perform specific actions or procedures on their behalf, and the Egyptian living abroad can inquire about the possibility of issuing the power of attorney through the Egyptian embassy or consulate in their country of residence.

### **Steps to Issue a Power of Attorney from the Egyptian Embassy or Consulate**

The organizational details, dates, and fees vary from one mission to another, but the usual procedures for the Egyptian citizen include the following steps:

1. Entering the official website of the Egyptian embassy or consulate in the country of residence and reviewing the power of attorney and declarations section.
2. Ensuring the required documents and the suitable form for the purpose of the power of attorney.
3. Preparing the original valid Egyptian ID and the required photos.
4. Preparing the attorney's data, the purpose of the power of attorney, and the authorities to be granted.
5. Booking an appointment if the mission requires prior booking.
6. Attending in person to sign before the relevant official according to the mission's procedures.
7. Reviewing the power of attorney data before endorsing and receiving it.

The Egyptian Ministry of Foreign Affairs explains that issuing powers of attorney for Egyptians requires submitting an original valid Egyptian ID, two copies of it, completing the power of attorney form, and signing before the relevant official. Additional documents may be required depending on the nature of the case.

### **Required Data to Prepare the Power of Attorney**

Before heading to the consulate, it is necessary to determine the data that will be included in the power of attorney, the most important of which are:

- The full name of the principal as stated in the ID.
- The attorney's data accurately.
- The purpose of the power of attorney, such as taking procedures to buy a property or signing a specific contract.
- The property data if it is known and specified at the time of issuing the power of attorney.
- The authorities allowed to the attorney to exercise.
- The duration of the power of attorney or the conditions of its termination, if the nature of the power of attorney allows it.

It is necessary to avoid using general phrases that do not reflect the real purpose of the delegation, and it is also necessary to review the wording with a legal expert if the transaction involves a sale, waiver, or disposal of property.

After receiving the power of attorney from the Egyptian mission, it must be sent to the agent inside Egypt in the manner accepted by the party before which the document will be used, and some parties may require the submission of the original document or an official copy, not just an electronic image.

Therefore, it is preferable to inquire in advance from the Real Estate Registry or the concerned party or the company owning the project about the accepted form of the document, so that the implementation of the procedure is not delayed due to differences in submission requirements.

## **What types of real estate powers of attorney are suitable for buying a property in Egypt?**

There is no one type of power of attorney that suits all real estate transactions; the choice depends on the nature of the procedure that the buyer wants to authorize someone else to perform, and it is important to distinguish between a special power of attorney and a general power of attorney, and between the powers of purchase and the powers of sale or disposal.

### **Special power of attorney in real estate transactions**

A special power of attorney is used when the buyer wants to specify a particular task for the agent, such as following up on the procedures for buying a specific unit or signing documents related to a specific transaction.

This type is characterized by the possibility of controlling the scope of authority instead of granting the agent broad authorization, and it can include specific procedures such as submitting applications or receiving documents or signing a contract, provided that the authorities are clearly stated and accepted by the concerned party.

### **General power of attorney and its limitations**

A general power of attorney grants broader powers according to its formulation, but it does not necessarily mean that the agent can perform all real estate transactions, as some transactions require a special and explicit text, and the concerned party may refuse to implement a procedure that is not covered by the power of attorney phrases.

Therefore, one should not rely on the name of the power of attorney alone, but rather read its full text and ensure that the powers contained therein are suitable for the intended purpose.

### **Power of purchase and power of sale and disposal**

It is necessary to distinguish between authorizing the agent to buy a property for the benefit of the principal, and granting him the authority to sell or dispose of the property after purchase.

The power of purchase focuses on the procedures related to completing the purchase process, while the powers of sale and disposal may include powers with greater legal and financial implications, and for this reason, it is not recommended to add powers of sale for oneself or for others, or to dispose of or authorize others except when actually required and after careful legal review.

## **How do you choose the right agent to complete the purchase of your property in Egypt?**

Choosing the right agent is one of the most important decisions made by the buyer residing abroad; because this person may deal with official documents or sign contracts or follow up on financial procedures on his behalf.

### **Criteria for selecting the person who represents you legally**

Before issuing the power of attorney, consider a set of basic criteria:

- Trust, honesty, and the ability to bear responsibility.
- The person's knowledge of the procedures they will be entrusted with.
- Clarity of the relationship between them and the buyer.
- The ability to provide reports and documents that prove what they have done.
- No conflict of interest between the agent and the buyer.
- Readiness to commit to the limits of the authorities stated in the power of attorney.

The choice should not be based solely on a family relationship; what is most important is the person's ability to execute the task with honesty and clarity.

### **Do you delegate a family member or a real estate lawyer?**

A family member may be suitable for following up on some simple procedures, such as receiving documents or visiting a certain entity.

However, if the transaction involves checking the property title or reviewing a complex contract or dealing with a potential dispute, it may be more appropriate to hire a lawyer specializing in real estate transactions.

In some cases, it is possible to combine the two roles: a trusted person to follow up on administrative matters, and an independent lawyer to review documents and legal formulations. Determining the authorities in the power of attorney remains necessary in both cases.

## **What authorities should be included in the real estate power of attorney?**

There is no one formula that suits all cases; the agent's authorities should be compatible with the nature of the property, the entity being dealt with, and the purpose of the authorization.

### **The basic information that the power of attorney should include**

It is best for the client's and agent's data to be clear and consistent with the identification documents, and for the power of attorney to specify its purpose without ambiguity. If the property is known, it is preferable to include its data to the extent allowed by the requirements of the competent authority.

### **Signing, payment, and document receipt authorities**

It must be specified whether the agent is authorized to:

- Submit requests and follow up on administrative procedures.
- Sign specific documents or contracts.
- Receive contracts, receipts, and official documents.
- Pay due amounts, if required and permitted.
- Deal with government agencies, the owning company, or the real estate developer.

If the agent is to be allowed to pay or receive money, this must be specified accurately, with a clear mechanism for proving payments and delivering receipts to the buyer.

### **Sales, waiver, and delegation authorities**

These authorities require special care; because they may enable the agent to take actions that go beyond mere purchase follow-up, such as selling to oneself or to others, waiving rights, and delegating someone else.

These clauses should not be added just because they exist in a ready-made model; there must be an actual need for them, and the client must understand their legal implications before signing.

## **How to document a power of attorney issued from abroad to be valid for use in Egypt**

The method of documenting the power of attorney depends on the entity that issued it and the country in which it was drawn up, and there is a difference between the power of attorney drawn up before an Egyptian mission and the power of attorney issued by a foreign notarization authority.

### **Documenting the power of attorney from the Egyptian embassy or consulate**

If the Egyptian citizen issues the power of attorney in front of the Egyptian embassy or consulate concerned, the mission's instructions regarding the form, signature, and required documents should be followed.

After receiving the power of attorney, it is necessary to ensure that the party to which it will be presented in Egypt accepts the document in the form in which it was issued, and whether it needs the original or an official copy or additional procedures.

### **Authentication of a power of attorney issued by a foreign entity**

If the power of attorney is issued in front of a notary public or a foreign documentation entity, it may need a series of authentications before it can be used in Egypt. The Egyptian Ministry of Foreign Affairs clarifies that foreign documents are authenticated by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs in the country of accreditation before being submitted to the Egyptian mission, with details varying according to the country and type of document.

There may be special requirements for translation if the power of attorney is written in a language other than Arabic; therefore, it is necessary to inquire with the concerned Egyptian mission about the correct procedure for authentication and translation before starting the procedures.

### **Conditions for accepting and using the power of attorney within Egypt**

Before sending the power of attorney to the agent, verify the following points:

- That it is issued by a competent authority.
- That the required authentications are complete.
- That the data of the client and the agent are clear.
- That the powers are consistent with the required procedure.
- That the power of attorney has not been canceled or its validity has not expired, if it is limited in time.
- That the document is accepted by the party in front of which it will be used.

Do not assume that every power of attorney issued from abroad is automatically accepted in all parties; the requirements for acceptance may vary according to the nature of the transaction and the competent authority.

## **Can a property be purchased in Egypt from abroad without issuing a power of attorney?**

Yes, the buyer may be able to complete some purchase procedures himself from abroad if the selling company or party provides means for remote dealing and accepts signatures and documents in the manner required by law, but this does not mean that all procedures can be completed electronically or without personal attendance.

### **Cases in which a purchase can be completed without an agent**

The buyer may not need a power of attorney if the procedures are limited to:

- Communicating with the seller and obtaining property data.
- Reviewing documents and sending comments.
- Conducting financial transfers through official channels.
- Signing documents that the competent authority accepts to be endorsed remotely.
- Following up on procedures electronically if the service is available.

It is necessary to ensure that the means of signing or documentation is legally acceptable to the authority handling the transaction.

### **When do you need to authorize a person inside Egypt?**

The presence of an agent may become necessary when the transaction requires attendance in front of a certain authority, or receiving original documents, or submitting requests that cannot be implemented remotely, or signing specific procedures on behalf of the buyer.

It is best to determine the tasks that the buyer cannot perform himself, and then issue a power of attorney that covers these tasks only, rather than issuing a broad authorization without need.

## **How to verify the validity of the power of attorney and property ownership before purchase?**

It is not enough to ensure the existence of a power of attorney to be assured of the safety of the transaction; it is necessary to verify its validity and the limits of the agent's powers, in addition to examining the property's ownership documents.

### **Ways to verify the validity and validity of the power of attorney**

You can review the power of attorney data and the entity that issued it, verify its number, date, parties, and the powers contained therein. It is also necessary to inquire with the competent authority about the method of verifying that it has not been canceled or that there is a procedure that affects its validity.

Do not rely on a picture sent by the agent alone if the transaction requires an original or official copy; rather, request the officially accepted document and verify it before completing any financial transaction.

### **How to examine the property ownership document and contract sequence**

You must review the document on which the seller relies to prove his ownership, then examine the transfer of ownership to him, especially if the property has been transferred between more than one owner.

It is important to review the names of the parties, property data, its area, boundaries, contract numbers, and dates, and ensure that there is no difference between the documents and the property offered for sale.

If the property belongs to a developer or administrative body, you must review the documents governing allocation, contracting, and waiver, and inquire with the competent authority about the conditions and restrictions that apply to the unit.

### **Verifying that the property is free from disputes and rights of others**

Before completing the purchase, it is necessary to examine whether the property is the subject of a dispute or linked to the rights of others, mortgages, restrictions, or financial obligations that have an impact, and this may require reviewing the competent authorities and seeking the assistance of a lawyer to examine the documents and verify the legal situation.

It is not recommended to transfer the full value of the property based on pictures of documents or verbal promises, but rather to link payments to clear stages and documents that prove each payment process.

## **The difference between the initial sales contract and the official registration of the property in Egypt**

One of the common mistakes is the belief that signing an initial sales contract or obtaining a judgment of signature validity necessarily means the transfer of property ownership and its official registration. The truth is that each procedure has a different legal nature.

### **What is the legal value of the initial sales contract?**

The initial sales contract proves the agreement between the two parties to the sale according to the conditions contained therein, and may impose legal obligations on the seller and the buyer. However, its impact on transferring ownership depends on the nature of the property, the ownership document, and the necessary legal procedures.

Therefore, it is necessary to examine the contract and the seller's ownership document and not be satisfied with the existence of a signed contract between the parties.

### **Does the validity of the signature prove the property ownership?**

A lawsuit to prove the validity of the signature aims primarily to prove the validity of the signature attributed to its owner on the document, and does not itself settle the validity of ownership or its transfer. Therefore, a judgment of signature validity should not be considered a substitute for registration or for examining the ownership document.

It is necessary to distinguish between the validity of the signature and the validity and enforceability of the contract, as each has a different legal nature and procedures and effects.

### **When do you need to register the property in the Real Estate Registry?**

Registering the real estate transaction is the procedure that should be researched when wanting to complete the transfer of ownership in accordance with the legal rules governing the Real Estate Registry, and the required documents and procedures differ according to the property's status, the basis of ownership, and the nature of the transaction.

Therefore, it is recommended to review a specialized lawyer or the concerned Real Estate Registry office to know the appropriate path for the property being purchased, and not to assume that all properties can be registered in the same way.

## **The Legal Procedures to be Reviewed Before Signing the Property Purchase Contract**

Before signing the purchase contract, it is necessary to review a set of details that may affect the buyer's rights or financial and legal obligations.

### **Verifying the Seller's Status and Ownership Document**

Ensure that the person signing the contract is the owner or the legal representative who has the authority to sell, and if the sale is being made through an agent, the power of attorney must be examined and it must be ensured that it includes the authority to sell and sign the required transaction.

### **Reviewing the Unit's Data and Contract Terms**

Review the property's description, area, location, unit number, floor, boundaries, facilities, and shared parts, and compare this data with the available documents. It is also necessary to ensure the clarity of the handover date, the unit's condition, and any terms related to finishing or maintenance.

### **Reviewing Financial Obligations and Handover Date**

The contract should clarify the total price, payment schedule, payment method, additional amounts, penalties for delay, and handover terms. It is also necessary to keep proof of all payments and not to hand over large amounts without clear documentation.

### **Ensuring the Agent's Authorities Before Signing the Contract**

If the signing will be done through an agent, the power of attorney text must be reviewed before setting the signing date. Ensure that the agent's authorities cover signing the specified contract and that they do not conflict with the terms of the owning or developing party or the concerned authority.

## **How to Amend or Cancel a Real Estate Power of Attorney from Outside Egypt**

The buyer's circumstances may change after issuing the power of attorney, and they may want to amend some authorities or terminate the authorization. In this case, official procedures must be followed, and it is not sufficient to inform the agent verbally.

### **When You Need to Issue a New Power of Attorney**

Issuing a new power of attorney may be more appropriate if the purpose of the authorization changes, or if there is a need to add an authority that was not present, or if the person who will perform the procedures changes.

No manual amendments should be made to the power of attorney after its adoption; the Egyptian Ministry of Foreign Affairs clarifies that deletion, amendment, or addition are not permitted after the power of attorney is issued and received.

### **Procedures for Canceling the Power of Attorney and Notifying the Agent**

The cancellation procedures differ according to the authority that issued the power of attorney and its nature. A citizen living abroad can review the concerned Egyptian embassy or consulate to know the possibility of canceling the power of attorney and the required documents.

It is important to document the cancellation in the official manner and notify the agent and the parties that previously used the power of attorney, according to the case, while keeping what proves the completion of the procedure.

### **What is the Impact of Canceling the Power of Attorney on Previous Transactions**

Canceling the power of attorney does not automatically mean canceling all transactions that took place before the cancellation; the legal effect depends on the timing of the transaction, its nature, and the extent of the validity of the procedures that took place. Therefore, if the agent has signed a contract or made a transaction before the cancellation, it is best to present the documents to a lawyer to determine the legal position.

## **Legal Mistakes to Avoid When Buying a Property in Egypt from Abroad**

Some mistakes can lead to financial losses or disputes that are difficult to deal with remotely, including:

### **Granting the Agent Broader Authorities than Necessary**

Granting the agent authorities for sale, waiver, receipt of funds, and power of attorney for others without actual need may expose the buyer to unnecessary risks. It is best to clearly define each task and authority.

### **Buying a Property without Checking Ownership Documents**

Relying on contract images or seller confirmations without legal review may lead to buying a property with restrictions, disputes, or ownership issues.

### **Relying on a Preliminary Contract without Legal Review**

Having a preliminary contract does not replace checking the property's status and possible registration procedures. It is necessary to know what the contract proves and what it does not prove before paying the property's value.

### **Transferring Funds without Official Evidence**

It is necessary to use traceable payment methods and keep receipts, transfers, and related correspondence. It is not recommended to transfer funds to accounts not associated with the contracting party without explanation and clear documentation.

## **Conclusion**

Knowing the powers of attorney and documents required to buy a property in Egypt from abroad helps the Egyptian living abroad organize the transaction and reduce the likelihood of disruption or falling into legal mistakes. This starts with preparing personal documents and property documents, then choosing the right agent and precisely defining their authorities, while ensuring the validity of the power of attorney and completing its documentation procedures.

Concern should not be limited to issuing the power of attorney only; checking the ownership deed, reviewing the sales contract, verifying the property's status, and documenting payments are all essential steps to protect the buyer's rights.

If you are planning to buy a property in Egypt from abroad, start by reviewing the documents and drafting the power of attorney with a legal specialist before signing or transferring funds.
